# NovaERP Module Map

## Platform Core

- Identity & access management
- Organization and membership
- Settings, platform workspace, integrations, and connector control plane
- Audit log and activity tracking
- Notification and chat
- File management

## Revenue & Operations

- Universal booking
- Hotel management
- Travel management
- Rental management
- Marketplace
- Payment orchestration

## Commercial & Customer

- CRM, Sales, and Order-to-Cash
- Customer portal and self-service
- Customer and vendor master data
- Helpdesk and support ticketing
- AI-generated communication

## Internal Operations

- HR
- Finance and accounting
- Procurement
- Warehouse
- Analytics and BI modeling
- Document control and knowledge workspace
- Fleet
- Project management
- Reporting and executive dashboards

## Delivery Strategy

- Tahap 1-3: arsitektur, folder structure, database baseline.
- Tahap 4-5: backend API dan authentication.
- Tahap 6-7: frontend shell dan dashboard.
- Tahap 8: implementasi module prioritas secara vertikal.
- Tahap 9-12: deployment, testing, optimization, README final.
