# ADR 019: Procurement Before Accounting

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Procurement flow dibangun penuh sampai purchase invoice preparation sebelum general ledger dan accounts payable final di Sprint 5.

## Consequences

- procurement dapat bergerak cepat tanpa menunggu accounting engine,
- invoice preparation menjadi boundary sementara,
- goods receipt dan supplier performance tetap bisa dianalisis lebih awal.
