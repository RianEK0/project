# ADR 023: Order-to-Cash Before AR Automation

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

NovaERP membangun fondasi sales order, fulfillment, invoice orchestration, return, credit note, customer credit, dan installment lebih dulu sebelum automation accounts receivable penuh.

## Consequences

- tim sales dapat bergerak sampai invoice dan return handling,
- warehouse dan invoice foundation existing dapat dipakai ulang,
- accounting automation tetap menjadi sprint lanjutan.
