# RFQ And Supplier Quotation

RFQ dipakai ketika organisasi ingin melakukan sourcing terkontrol.

## Flow

1. RFQ dibuat dari satu atau lebih purchase request.
2. RFQ dikirim ke supplier terpilih.
3. Supplier quotation diterima.
4. Quotation dibandingkan berdasarkan harga, lead time, kualitas, dan SLA historis.
5. Vendor comparison menghasilkan rekomendasi supplier.

## Comparison Dimensions

- unit price,
- quoted lead time,
- historical on-time rate,
- quality acceptance rate,
- minimum order quantity,
- contract or blanket-order eligibility.

## Output

- shortlist supplier,
- recommendation score,
- award decision,
- handoff ke purchase order.
