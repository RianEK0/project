# Sales Quotation and Pipeline Foundation

## Sales Quotation

Quotation menjadi dokumen komersial sebelum invoice atau order fulfillment formal.

Status utama:

- `DRAFT`
- `SENT`
- `VIEWED`
- `NEGOTIATING`
- `ACCEPTED`
- `REJECTED`
- `EXPIRED`
- `CONVERTED`
- `CANCELLED`

## Pipeline

Pipeline menyajikan nilai open business menurut stage:

- `LEAD`
- `OPPORTUNITY`
- `QUOTATION`
- `NEGOTIATION`
- `WON`
- `LOST`

## Summary Metrics

- total open count,
- total open value,
- weighted open value,
- stage mix,
- stalled stage highlight untuk tindak lanjut cepat.
