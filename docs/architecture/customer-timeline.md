# Customer Timeline Foundation

Customer timeline menyatukan event CRM/Sales lintas lead, opportunity, deal, dan aktivitas.

## Event Types

- lead created,
- lead qualified,
- opportunity advanced,
- deal updated,
- call logged,
- email sent,
- WhatsApp sent,
- quotation sent,
- task created,
- follow up scheduled,
- meeting held.

## Goals

- memberi konteks cepat ke sales rep saat membuka customer,
- menyatukan komunikasi manual dan progres komersial,
- menyiapkan fondasi customer 360 sebelum helpdesk dan automation masuk.
