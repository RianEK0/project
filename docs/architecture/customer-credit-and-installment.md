# Customer Credit and Installment Foundation

## Customer Credit

Customer credit membaca exposure dari order terbuka, invoice terbuka, dan overdue balance terhadap limit customer.

Risk level starter:

- `AVAILABLE`
- `WATCHLIST`
- `ON_HOLD`
- `BLOCKED`

## Installment

Installment plan membagi tagihan menjadi beberapa termin.

Status utama:

- `DRAFT`
- `ACTIVE`
- `PARTIALLY_PAID`
- `PAID`
- `OVERDUE`
- `DEFAULTED`
- `CANCELLED`
